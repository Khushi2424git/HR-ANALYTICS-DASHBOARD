# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Excel

## Project Overview
An Excel-based HR Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze employee data and visualize workforce trends, attrition, salary, age demographics, and business travel patterns.
<img width="1330" height="663" alt="DASHBOARD" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d0b648d-36b8-408e-b0b6-b4c9703390a7" />

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze employee headcount and attrition.
- Understand workforce distribution across departments.
- Explore employee demographics and salary trends.
- Present key HR metrics through visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas and KPI Calculations
- Data Analysis & Visualization

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators
- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Current Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations
- Attrition by Department
- Workforce by Department
- Business Travel by Department
- Employee Age Distribution
- Employee Status
- Average Monthly Income

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset contains **1,470 employee records and 44 columns**, including department, job role, attrition, salary, age, job satisfaction, business travel, and career development information.

## 🧮 Attrition Rate Formula

```excel
=COUNTIF(tblHR[Attrition],"Yes")/COUNTA(tblHR[Employee Number])
```

Format the result as a percentage.
